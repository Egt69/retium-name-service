Retium Name Service (RNS)

Independent project notice: RNS is an independent project built on the Retium testnet. It is not an official Retium project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Retium, the Retium Foundation, or the Retium core team.

1. About this project

RNS is an independent, voluntary project built on the Retium testnet. It is not an official Retium project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Retium, the Retium Foundation, or the Retium core team.

You are welcome to try it at your own discretion. This page explains what RNS is, what you can expect, and the risks you take when you use it.

2. What RNS is

RNS is a non-custodial naming and marketplace application. It lets you register, renew, update, transfer, list, and purchase .rtm names using a self-custody wallet. RNS does not hold your funds, names, or private keys. All actions are executed by smart contracts on the Retium testnet after you sign them in your wallet.

3. Your wallet and your responsibility

  • You are solely responsible for your wallet, devices, recovery phrase, and every transaction you sign.
  • Blockchain transactions are final. RNS cannot reverse, cancel, or refund a transaction.
  • RNS will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not RNS.
  • Always check the transaction details in your wallet before approving.

4. Names

  • No ownership guarantee. A name is a record in a smart contract. Registering a name does not give you rights in any brand, person, or organisation.
  • Expiry. Names expire at the time shown on-chain. After expiry a name may be registered by someone else. Renewing is your responsibility.
  • First come, first served. Names are assigned by the contract on a first-come basis. RNS does not decide who should own a name.
  • Testnet resets. Because the Service runs on a testnet, every name, whether you registered it, bought it, or received it by transfer, together with its records, listings, and history, may be lost if the network or contracts are reset. RNS does not promise to restore lost names or to refund or compensate you for them.

5. Acceptable use

Please do not use the Service to:

  • Impersonate a person, project, or organisation, or register names to mislead others.
  • Register names to extort or resell to the owner of a brand or identity (name squatting in bad faith).
  • Register or display names or avatars that are hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative, or that harm others.
  • Attack, overload, scrape abusively, or interfere with the Service, its contracts, or the network.
  • Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities for gain instead of reporting them (see Section 9).
  • Interfere with other users or the project.

Because names live on a public blockchain, RNS cannot delete them. We may hide or de-list a name in our interface if it breaks these rules. This affects only what the website shows, not the on-chain record.

6. Marketplace

  • Sellers set their own prices. Buyers must check the price, the name, and its expiry before purchase.
  • RNS is not involved in sales between users and does not guarantee the value or quality of any name.
  • Registration fees are calculated by the contract and paid to the treasury shown in the app. Marketplace sellers set their own prices and receive payment directly. Any fees are shown before you confirm.
  • Testnet assets may have no monetary value. Do not treat testnet activity as an investment. A name you buy may disappear in a testnet reset, and the price paid will not be refunded.

7. Third-party services

The Service depends on third parties such as the Retium Web Wallet, RPC providers, block explorers, and hosting. RNS does not control them and is not responsible for their downtime, errors, or policies.

8. Branding and source code

RNS is an independent project. Its name, website design, text, and branding are maintained by a-kai. The source repository is available at github.com/Egt69/retium-name-service. "Retium" and related marks are used only to describe the network and remain with their respective owners.

9. Security reports

If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately through Discord a_kai.me or X @cryptoyef, and allow a reasonable time to fix it before public disclosure. Do not exploit it or access other people's assets. Never include a seed phrase or private key in a report.

10. Changes, suspension, and termination

The Service may change, pause, or end at any time without notice, including during testnet resets or migration to another network. We may update this page from time to time; the latest version is shown here.

11. Risk and responsibility

You use RNS entirely at your own risk. RNS is an independent, voluntary project, and you are responsible for anything that happens when you use it. You accept the consequences of:

  • Data leaks or exposure of information connected to your wallet, browser, or device.
  • Real financial loss, including lost funds, fees, or the cost of names.
  • Lost, expired, reset, or transferred names and records.
  • Wallet compromise, signing mistakes, or user error.
  • Bugs, outages, network resets, or third-party failures.

RNS does not promise to restore data or names, refund fees, compensate you, or cover any loss. If you are not comfortable with these risks, do not use the Service.
